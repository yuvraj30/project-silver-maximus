Bellwood Health System
Board paper, FY25

A trusted health system.
A $69M margin challenge.

Situation
Three hospitals and 46 clinics, $2.3B of revenue, and referrers who rate the care well.
Complication
The margin is 4% against a 7% target: $69M a year short, with 18 months and $35M to act.
Question
What can Bellwood credibly commit to now, and what must wait for proof?

The answer. Approve $32.8M now, within the $35M cap, and hold $12.2M behind gates. Bellwood can commit to 4.6% by month 18 from labor, administration and sourcing, and earn 5.6% at month 18 and 6.0% by month 30 as gated levers pass proof. If the hospital program is not run, Base reaches 5.47% at month 30. The Base case ends $23.9M a year short of 7%.

The bridge to 7%. The last $23.9M (Base, month 30) closes with two named levers and their proof. First, the hospital program at its High set ($32.8M gross a year): discharge flow (CMO and CNO), emergency flow (COO and ED director), revenue integrity (CFO), clinical supply (CFO and surgeon committee), each released at Gate 1 only if the diagnostic shows avoidable days above 4% of patient days, occupancy and boarding hours, a denial write-off rate above 2% of net revenue, and clinical supply spend outside the $180M pool. Second, High-case labor, administration and sourcing: agency labor taken to the $85M FY23 floor (CFO and CNO), administrative AI at 1.5x (COO), sourcing at 1.5x (CFO), each reconciled to the ledger at every gate. Together they reach 7.16% at month 30, and High-case execution reaches 7% in about month 21.
